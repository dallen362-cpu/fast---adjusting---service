Good To Know

Questions Homeowners Ask Us

What exactly does a public adjuster do?

A public adjuster is a licensed professional who represents you — the policyholder — instead of the insurance company. We document the damage, interpret your policy, file the claim, and negotiate directly with your carrier to get you the maximum settlement you're entitled to.

How do I get a phone number so fast?

FAST Connect™ runs a Smart DID engine that provisions numbers programmatically. When you join, it requests a number from the carrier pool, assigns it to a new royalty account under your name, registers a STIR/SHAKEN-verified branded caller ID, and turns on AI answering and call-tracking — all through an API in under a second, with no human interaction. The number is live and routable the moment it appears in your app.

How does the FAST Connect™ number & royalty pay work?

When you join, we issue you a unique company phone number inside your private-labeled app. That number is your agent ID and royalty account — anyone who calls, texts, or submits a mold, water, fire, or storm job through it is automatically credited to you. When the claim is recovered and the property is restored, your royalty is calculated and paid out through the app, all tracked in real time. Royalty terms are provided at sign-up.

How much does the claim service cost?

Nothing upfront. We work on a contingency basis — our fee is a percentage of the settlement we recover for you. If we don't recover anything, you owe us nothing.

My claim was already denied. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Denied and underpaid claims are one of our specialties. We re-inspect, re-document, and re-negotiate — often reversing outcomes the carrier called final.

How fast can you inspect my property?

For active storm damage we respond 24/7 and can often inspect the same or next day. Use the calendar above or call our Emergency line to get on the schedule immediately.

Are you licensed in Florida?

Yes. FAST Adjusting Service Team Corp is an active, state-registered Florida corporation (Document P18000041519) based in Cutler Bay, serving policyholders across South Florida.
